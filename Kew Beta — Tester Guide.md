# Kew Beta — Getting Started

Thanks for testing Kew! Here's everything you need to get up and running.

---

## What is Kew?

Kew replaces your algorithmic YouTube feed with a queue you control. You pick the videos, watch them intentionally, and earn skips by finishing what you start.

---

## Step 1 — Download Expo Go

Kew runs inside an app called **Expo Go**, which lets you test early-stage apps without going through the App Store.

- **iPhone:** [Download Expo Go on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/expo-go/id982107779)
- **Android:** [Download Expo Go on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=host.exp.exponent)

---

## Step 2 — Open the Kew link

Once Expo Go is installed, tap the link I sent you. It will open Expo Go and load Kew automatically. This may take 10–20 seconds the first time while the app downloads.

If tapping doesn't work, open Expo Go manually and paste the link into the search bar at the top.

---

## Step 3 — Sign in with Google

Kew uses your Google account to read your YouTube subscriptions — nothing else. It can't post, upload, or modify anything on your account.

Tap **Continue with Google**, choose your account, and you're in.

---

## A few things to know

**Building your queue:** Head to the Browse tab to see videos from your YouTube subscriptions. Tap the **+** button to add any video to your queue.

**Watching:** Tap your queue entry to open the player. Videos play in order — finish one to unlock the next.

**Skips:** You start with 3 skips. Use them to move a video to the end of your queue if you're not feeling it. You earn one back each time you finish a video.

**The app may feel slow on first load** — this is normal for a test build. It'll be faster in a finished version.
